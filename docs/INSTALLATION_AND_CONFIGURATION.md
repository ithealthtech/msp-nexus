# Installation and configuration

## Install

1. In WordPress, open Appearance > Themes > Add New > Upload Theme and upload `msp-nexus.zip`.
2. Activate MSP Nexus. The public site remains usable without the companion plugin.
3. Open Plugins > Add New > Upload Plugin, upload `msp-nexus-core.zip`, and activate it.
4. Open MSP Nexus > Diagnostics and resolve failed environment checks.
5. Open MSP Nexus > Settings and enter verified organization, contact, support, social, integration, and update preferences.
6. Optionally open MSP Nexus > Setup, review the production-safe starter manifest, confirm a backup, and process resumable batches. Generic business pages publish; unverified proof, people, resources, events, relationships, careers, and legal placeholders stay in Draft.

The importer never runs automatically. License activation is optional for installed public content; it controls only entitled downloads, updates, and vendor services.

## Site editing

Use Appearance > Editor for templates, template parts, navigation, styles, and patterns. Save a site backup before editing global templates. WordPress revisions and the editor's revision history are the primary recovery path. Use the child theme for PHP or durable code-level changes.

## Production checklist

- Review every draft labeled sample, fictional, placeholder, or professional review required before publishing it.
- Create a real navigation menu, privacy notice, cookie treatment, accessibility statement, support route, and contact consent language.
- Verify phone numbers, addresses, claims, awards, partner marks, pricing, service commitments, dates, and legal terms.
- Configure HTTPS, backups, transactional email, spam controls, caching, image optimization, monitoring, and least-privilege administrator accounts at the host/platform layer.
- Activate optional integrations only after reviewing their data flows and vendor terms.
