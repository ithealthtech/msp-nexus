# Commercial entitlement terms template

This editable business template is not legal advice and is separate from the GPL-2.0-or-later licenses governing distributed WordPress code. Qualified counsel must adapt it.

Define: contracting parties; covered product/service identifiers; subscription term; permitted production, staging, local, multisite, and agency usage; activation allowance; customer portal access; support scope and response targets; update and download eligibility; renewal, cancellation, suspension, revocation, refund, and transfer rules; taxes; acceptable use; security responsibilities; service availability; data processing and retention; confidentiality; warranties and disclaimers; liability limits; indemnities; export/sanctions terms; governing law; notice route; and order-of-precedence rules.

Expiration or service outage must not disable already installed public content. It may stop future premium downloads, hosted services, and support after the disclosed grace behavior. The customer must be able to export site-owned content without an active entitlement.
